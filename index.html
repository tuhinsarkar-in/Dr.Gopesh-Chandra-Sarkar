Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar | PhD Botany Homeopathy West Bengal

  • Ex-Reader at University College (North Bengal University) Raiganj, Uttar Dinajpur District, West Bengal
  • BJP Candidate Raiganj 2009: Lok Sabha candidate for the Raiganj Constituency during the 2009 Indian General Elections
  • Former National Council Member of the BJP
  • Former State Vice-President of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) in West Bengal
  • Former State Vice-President of the Jatiyatabadi Adhyapak O Gabeshak Sangha (ABRSM) in West Bengal
  • Former Sanghachalak (President) of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) in West Dinajpur District
  • Former Sanghachalak (President) of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) in Uttar Dinajpur District
  • Former President of the Arya Sangha, founded by Swami Samadhi Prakash Aranya Maharaj
  • Suffered Imprisonment in 1992, as the prime accused during the Ram Mandir Movement at Ayodhya, on false allegations by the then leftist government led by Mr. Jyoti Basu in West Bengal.

Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar was a dedicated scholar, a strong nationalist, and a lifelong learner. With a PhD in Botany and being a self-taught homeopath, he was widely respected for his calm strength, clear thinking, and deep commitment to education and service. He valued hard work, honesty, and helping others, earning admiration in both academic and community circles. Beyond his academic and political pursuits, he had a wide range of interests including music, magic, art, yoga and spiritualism, medical science, driving, rifle shooting (through the NCC), photography, telegraphy, astrology, etc. He actively participated in social service programs and maintained a profound inclination toward the spiritual world. Even after retirement, his patriotic wisdom, principled life, and quiet sacrifices continued to inspire everyone around him.

SMT. Khela Sarkar (Alias Jaya Sarkar)

Shreemati Khela Sarkar, also known by her alias Jaya Sarkar, is the spouse of Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar. Her life has been defined by her unwavering dedication to the Rashtra Sevika Samiti and her deep passion for music.

  • Rashtra Sevika Samiti: Served as a devoted worker (Vibhag Sanchalika) under the name Jaya Sarkar. She actively participated in programs (Prashikshan Shivir), presented patriotic songs, and shared the stage with prominent leaders like Hon. Venkatramaiah Shantha Kumari ("Shanthakka"), the Pramukh Sanchalika (Chief).
  • Academic Excellence: Holds a double-graduate degree, having completed her Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Music.
  • Musical Achievements: Stood second at the All India Sangeet Pravakar Exam in 1985, winning a silver medal. She is proficient in playing the harmonium and Tanpura, and skilled in singing both classical and folk music.
  • Prominent Meetings: An ardent follower of legendary musician Hemanta Mukhopadhyay, she had the honor of meeting him alongside Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar in 1984.

Timeline

1. Early Life & Education (1941-1970)

Born on January 1st, 1941, in a modest village in Pabna District (Betil) in undivided Bengal, Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar grew up with an insatiable curiosity for science and spirituality. He pursued higher education with dedication and completed his PhD in Botany in the 1970s, laying the foundation for a lifelong journey of learning.

2. Academic & Teaching Career (1970–2003)

After earning his PhD, he began a long and respected teaching career at University College (North Bengal University, Raiganj), inspiring thousands of students over 30 years. He also had a keen interest in medical science - allopathy, ayurveda, homoeopathy, naturopathy, etc. He retired in 2003, leaving a lasting impact through his clear thinking, honesty, and deep knowledge.

3. RSS Worker & MP Candidate (2003–2010)

He was a dedicated member of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS). He was invited to join RSS in the 1980s because of his devotion towards Hindu philosophy and research on spirituality. He was also the founder of the spiritual research organisation named Sandhanichakra. He followed the RSS values of discipline, service, and nationalism. Over the years, he became the RSS President (Sanghachalak) of West Dinajpur District (WB). His commitment to public service led him into active politics, where he served as the BJP West Bengal State Vice-President. In 2009, he contested in the Lok Sabha election from the Raiganj constituency as a BJP candidate, campaigning passionately for his ideals and love for the nation, despite limited resources and support.

4. Legacy & Final Milestones (2010–Present)

In his later years, Dr. Sarkar focused on securing his family’s future. He made sure both his sons were well-settled, sending Uttam Sarkar to Bangalore for higher studies and Goutam Sarkar to Kolkata for vocational training. He also supported their marriages: Goutam’s marriage to Priyanka in 2007, and Uttam’s marriage to Moumita in 2008. When his grandsons were born - Tuhin Sarkar in 2010 and Rishav Sarkar in 2012—his role as a guide in the family grew stronger. In December 2020, during the pandemic, he bought a new home and moved to Barasat on 7th April 2021, near the Kolkata airport, selling his Raiganj property. When the family visited the Barasat home in December 2021, it felt like a major milestone—an 80-year-old opening a new chapter for himself and the next generation.

World Famous Magician, P.C. Sorcar Jr.

Dr. Sarkar had an indomitable and inherent curiosity and numerous queries, from his boyhood, about the miracles of the mysterious spiritual world and the supernatural powers claimed to have been achieved by yogis and tantrics. Through his parapsychological research organization, *Sandhani Chakra*, he aimed to unveil and assess the reality of these claims and reveal the truth behind them. To facilitate such research work, he felt the need to acquire proper knowledge of magic tricks in addition to the scientific expertise of the scholars in his Sandhani Team. Therefore, he came into contact with the world-famous magician "Jadu Samrat" P.C. Sorcar (Senior) in Kolkata, and being inspired and guided by the great P.C. Sorcar (Sr.), he learned basic magic tricks from the Indian Magic Institute, Kolkata—not to become a professional magician, but to conduct proper investigations into the claimed supernatural activities. Interestingly, his very name, G.C. Sarkar, seemed magical and, perhaps, required some knowledge of magic to truly be worthy of it.

Dr. G.C Sarkar is collecting spiritual information from Maha Yogi Swami Somadhi Prakash Aranya, Maharaj

Swami Samadhi Prakash Aranya Maharaj (1885–1985) was a renowned Sankhya-Yogi of Bengal, a highly educated, enlightened, and divine personality who achieved *Moksha* (Nirvana)—the highest goal of yoga-sadhana—in the middle part of his long lifespan of 101 years. He attained immense yogic and supernatural powers early in life, and through deep meditation, he reached a transcendental stage where he could recall his previous lives, thereby revealing and supporting the truth of the Hindu doctrine of rebirth (reincarnation). Following an accidental head injury that resulted in multiple skull fractures, he was admitted to a hospital where surgery and anaesthesia were deemed inevitable. However, he instructed the doctor to proceed without anaesthesia, and the operation was carried out accordingly while he remained completely calm and quiet throughout, astonishing the doctor, who declared him to be undoubtedly a powerful yogi with supernatural abilities. Most incredibly, his divine touch reportedly revived the dead bodies of a young girl and a baby. Beyond his yogic power, he was also a great writer, social reformer, and yoga researcher, participating in the Indian Science Congress Association’s conferences in Hyderabad (1937) and Kolkata (1938). He strongly opposed the evil practice of untouchability. Dr. Gopesh Chandra felt fortunate to become a humble disciple of this great Yogi.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Historical Archive

Who was Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar? ▼

Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar (1941–2023) was an Indian academic, botanist (PhD), educator, and political leader who served as the State Vice-President of the BJP in West Bengal, RSS Sanghachalak of West Dinajpur, and contested the 2009 Lok Sabha elections from Raiganj constituency.

Which political party did he represent in the 2009 Raiganj election? ▼

Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar represented the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) as its official candidate from the Raiganj Lok Sabha constituency in West Bengal during the 2009 Indian General Elections, securing nearly 40,000 votes.

What were his academic & scientific contributions? ▼

Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar earned his PhD in Botany in the 1970s and served as a University Reader at University College (North Bengal University, Raiganj) for over 30 years. His publications explored plant physiology, homeopathy, and Kirlian bio-photography.

What was Dr. Sarkar's role in the RSS and BJP? ▼

Dr. Sarkar served as the Sanghachalak (District President) of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) for West Dinajpur and Uttar Dinajpur, and as State Vice-President of the BJP in West Bengal, hosting RSS Sarsanghchalak Rajju Bhaiya at his Raiganj residence.

What is Sandhanichakra and who founded it? ▼

Sandhanichakra was a spiritual and parapsychological research organisation founded and headed by Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar to investigate rebirth, reincarnation, past lives, and yogic phenomena through systematic scientific observation.

Where was Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar born? ▼

Dr. Gopesh Chandra Sarkar was born on January 1, 1941, in the village of Betil, Pabna District in undivided Bengal (now Bangladesh), navigating the historical partition before relocating to India in the 1960s.

The survey clearly reveals that Dr. Sarkar possessed a wide range of interests beyond his professional excellence in academia. Alongside his scholarly achievements, he was deeply engaged in political, social, and cultural spheres. His passions spanned politics, music, magic, art, photography, boxing, driving, rifle shooting, spiritualism, etc. Dr. Sarkar truly defied the old saying, “Jack of all trades, master of none”. He was indeed a jack of many trades but master of science and held a doctorate in science as well.
